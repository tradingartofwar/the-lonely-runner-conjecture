WHAT THE CUT PRESERVES

Every surviving point still meets the old constraints.

For each seventh lap k, the child is exactly the parent intersected with k + z ≤ 5x + 2y ≤ k + 1 − z. Adding a constraint removes points. It can also create new boundary edges and contacts among the points that remain.

WHAT MUST STAY DISTINCT

Ambient survival, physical survival, complete survival.

A child can be nonempty yet miss the actual orbit. An isolated physical point can be safe despite having zero duration. A surviving optimizer can lie outside all old edges. Each question needs the corresponding information.

Exact parent/child vertices and incidences come from the pinned atlas. Orbit slices are constructed by rational edge intersections and checked independently against the sliced inequalities.
