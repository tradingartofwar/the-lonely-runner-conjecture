WHY THE FIRST CONTACT MATTERS

Less loss means more separation.

The largest possible separation in the fixed geometry is 1/6. As the section descends, e increases and z = 1/6 − e decreases. The earliest integer contact across the seven caps therefore gives the highest physically compatible separation.

The pinned proof candidate supplies witnesses strictly above 1/7. That is why geometry below this cut cannot improve the B-ray optimum.
