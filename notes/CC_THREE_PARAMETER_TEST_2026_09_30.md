## 1. What changed, and what independence means

The speeds are

\[
(0,p,q,p+q,2p+q,3p+q,3p+2q,6p+2q,3p+8q,r),
\qquad p,q,r\in\mathbb Z_{>0}.
\]

The previous last speed, generated by p,q, is replaced by a free r. Runner
count, the other eight moving forms, common start, stationary reference and
closed 1/8 structural target remain fixed. Ten distinct speeds require
p!=q, p!=2q, and r unequal to each of the other moving speeds. A returned
1/8-safe time exceeds the ten-runner requirement 1/10.

The coefficient matrix contains the rows (1,0,0),(0,1,0),(0,0,1), with
determinant one, so its family rank is three. No fixed formula r=ap+bq is
imposed. Nevertheless each particular integer-speed instance has rational
rank one and a one-dimensional periodic orbit. Parameter rank, ambient torus
dimension and orbit dimension must remain distinct. This does not test
rationally independent irrational speeds.

The extension is deliberately minimal: eight forms still use p,q; only the
ninth uses r. The ambient safe set therefore has product structure in the
new coordinate. Success here cannot be silently extended to families having
several mixed forms involving r.

TRAIN comprises all 89 primitive, distinct-speed triples in [1,6]^3. HOLDOUT
comprises the 318 such triples in [1,9]^3 outside that smaller cube. Menus
are selected on TRAIN before HOLDOUT is evaluated. These are deterministic
frozen domains, not random or blind samples. Six doubled/base pairs supply
12 additional scaling invocations, with overlap explicitly excluded from
the 407-configuration denominator.

## 3. The relationship that replaces one scalar contact

Normalize (p,q,r)=g(A,B,C), gcd(A,B,C)=1. Let

\[
d=\gcd(A,B),\quad P=A/d,\quad Q=B/d,
\quad aP+bQ=1,\quad cd+eC=1.
\]

The proposed exact orbit condition for a point in [0,1)^3 is

\[
h=Qx-Py\in\mathbb Z,
\qquad n=C(ax+by)-dz\in\mathbb Z. \tag{1}
\]

Both must hold at the same point. Their coefficient rows have cross product
(A,B,C), rather than a nonprimitive multiple. This preserves the full integer
relation lattice rather than admitting extra components.

Here is a direct sufficiency argument, without relying only on that lattice
description. Set w=ax+by and U=cw+ez. Then

\[
Pw=x-bh,\quad Qw=y+ah,\quad dU=w-en,\quad CU=z+cn.
\]

Consequently

\[
AU=x-bh-Pen,\quad BU=y+ah-Qen,\quad CU=z+cn.
\]

All corrections are integers. Thus tau={U}, t=tau/g recovers exactly
(x,y,z)=({pt},{qt},{rt}). Necessity follows by substituting an actual orbit
point into (1). For N=floor(U), the coordinate physical laps are

\[
L_p=-bh-Pen-AN,\quad L_q=ah-Qen-BN,\quad L_r=cn-CN.
\]

An old row (alpha,beta), with torus lap m, has physical lap
m+alpha L_p+beta L_q. The direct speed multiplication in the saved
certificates checks these phases and laps, including the new r coordinate.

Projecting a sheet through (h,n) gives a parallelogram, possibly degenerate.
The frozen exact solver enumerates integer h slices, then checks the n
interval on the SAME slice. Constant h, points and equality cases are handled
separately. This is the additional compatibility information absent from one
scalar projection interval.

The old positive-width argument did not automatically become a rank-three
finite-exception theorem. The two projected coordinate ranges can each
contain integers without their joint image containing an integer pair.
No infinite-domain reduction is claimed in this experiment.

## 5. Three exact information-loss counterexamples

**Separate projection ranges lose the common point.** At (1,2,6), take
S=[(1/8,1/4),(11/72,5/24)] and z=7/8. The ranges are
h in [0,7/72] and n in [-1/8,1/24]. Both contain the integer zero, but
h=0 requires source parameter s=0 while n=0 requires s=3/4. There is no
common integer contact. A bounding rectangle of the joint projection gives
a false positive. Keep the actual projected line/parallelogram or its slices.

**Two raw integer conditions can still admit false orbit components.** For
(p,q,r)=(2,3,1), the safe ambient point (1/4,7/8,1/8) satisfies
3x-2y=-1 and x-2z=0. The proper second relation is -x+y-z=1/2, which is
not integral. Since r=1, a physical point with z=1/8 must have t=1/8 modulo
one, giving y=3/8 rather than 7/8. The raw pair conditions were insufficient
despite every ambient phase being safe. Their cross product is twice the
primitive speed vector; the missing saturation is consequential information.

**The old canonical pair time loses a valid lift.** At (2,4,5), the sheet
returns (3/8,3/4,7/16). Recovering only the old pair clock's j=0 branch
gives time 3/16; the new r phase is 15/16, unsafe at 1/8 and at 1/10.
Keeping the other lift gives t=11/16 and r phase 7/16. The first eight
phases remain safe and unchanged. The saved record retains d=2, lift index
one, both integer contacts, all labels and the physical recovery.

These failures concern different information: joint location, the complete
integer relations, and the multiplicity of physical clocks. A list of safe
marginal phases does not preserve any of these automatically.

## 7. CC checkpoint and next task

What survives is the method of attaching shared safety geometry, lap labels,
exact integer contact and physical recovery to a supported question. Moving
from segments in a two-torus to sheets in a three-torus keeps that structure
usable. The arithmetic must carry two saturated relations and the additional
clock lifts. Candidate discovery still finds a compact local menu.

What does not survive automatically is uniform coverage by the selected menu,
the old scalar-width finite-exception proof, or the assumption that safe
projection marginals identify a compatible physical configuration. In this
test the source sheets retain enough alternatives; compression into four
training-selected sheets discards eight needed held-out choices.

No full three-dimensional safe cell was needed for the reached cases. This
does not rule out that need for other rows or new questions. The full safe set,
optimal time, every maximizer, other references and unrestricted rank-three
families remain outside this certificate's contract.

**Next proposed:** derive a coverage criterion for the joint integer-contact
image of a sheet, using these eight misses as fixed development counterexamples.
Then freeze a new selection rule and genuinely fresh validation domain before
testing it. Do not rerank on this holdout and call the repaired result a
successful transfer. The earlier two-coordinate coefficient-progression
certificate and coefficient-aware dispatcher remain separately pending.
