# Configuration-level relational information

September 28, 2026. Preserved from Vance's explicit research brief before the next calculation. Status: OPEN methodological inquiry; the linked mathematical findings retain their own evidence levels. Material AI involvement: organization, qualification and the proposed operational test.

## The idea to carry forward

Some facts concern relationships among parts, rather than properties of any one part. If Jon and Sam are each booked Monday, Wednesday and Friday, the statement that their schedules coincide is a fact about their comparison. It is determined by the two complete schedules; it is not additional independently supplied data.

The Lonely Runner counterpart is the joint trajectory

`(v_1 t,...,v_k t) mod 1`

under a common clock and common start. Speeds, blocking fractions, meeting counts and pairwise overlap totals can be useful summaries while hiding decisive joint restrictions.

Two questions from the brief should remain explicit:

> What information exists only in the relationships among the runners, and is therefore invisible when we summarize runners or pairs separately?

> What configuration-level relational invariant or constraint distinguishes complete-looking blocking schedules from ones that must leave a lonely moment?

The central methodological statement is:

> The missing information may not be absent from the mathematics; it may be absent from our representation.

“Relational” here describes what the fact is about and which summaries expose it. It does not mean the full speed vector is mathematically insufficient, or that the runners causally influence one another.

## Candidate structures

- Relative phase alignment and its common-start constants.
- Placement of overlap inside openings left by other runners.
- Repeated timing relationships across laps and windows.
- Simultaneous or higher-order blocking.
- Compatibility of several schedules at the same time.
- Integer or residue relations among multiple speeds.
- Direction and timing of entering or leaving safety at equality contacts.
- Constraints on a whole configuration that cannot be assigned to one runner alone.

An invariant must specify its transformation group: reflection, speed scaling, time translation, relabelling or a particular deformation. A useful relational constraint need not be an invariant. Do not call every new statistic an invariant.

## How to use this in the research

1. Name the target: existence, positive duration, exact duration, contacts, or certificate discovery.
2. State exactly which summary is retained. Do not say two inputs have “the same information” when only chosen moments match.
3. Exhibit a difference in the target, or an exact abstract covering countermodel that shows what the summary still permits. Keep those two evidence types distinct.
4. Restore one declared relational constraint from the actual speeds and shared clock.
5. Test whether it rules out all relevant false covers, rather than only the first cover found.
6. Account for the cost of deriving it; a small scalar can conceal a large reconstruction.
7. Preserve a failure and the tight/equality controls. A failed sufficient condition is not failed loneliness.

The next bounded operational test is a common time displacement from existing rational anchors. A shared interval exists exactly when the latest entry does not exceed the earliest exit. This is a joint compatibility condition, derived before the final allowed set is consulted. It extends the earlier directional-equality and adaptive-witness reasoning without claiming to invent interval intersection.

The subsequent [direct lap selector](../DIRECT_LAP_PROJECTION_2026_09_28.md) turns the occurrence question into arithmetic. On a supplied core-safe window, two residual runners need at most four next-safe-time projections; a separately marked follow-on gives ten nested projections for three at threshold 1/8. The useful relational guarantee is that the remaining constraints cannot push that time beyond a fresh safe interval. A naive two-sweep treatment of three runners fails, while grouping two as an earliest-joint-safe operation repairs the same example. This preserves a concrete distinction between an arbitrary iteration order and a proved timing bound. It gives conditional local selection, not a new invariant or a proof that every full configuration supplies a successful window.
