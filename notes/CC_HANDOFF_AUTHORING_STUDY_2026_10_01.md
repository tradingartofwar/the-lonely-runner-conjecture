# Can CC help decide what survives a handoff?

October 1, 2026, America/Los_Angeles. **Reviewed and frozen; execution attempt closed before participant delivery.**

The [completed handoff pilot](CC_HANDOFF_PILOT_2026_10_01.md) found no robust advantage from CC-informed presentation when both formats received identical factual statements. The question left open concerns authoring: can the procedure help choose and preserve consequential information from a longer history?

The [new study package](../reviews/2026-10-01-cc-handoff-authoring/README.md) now makes that question testable on six synthetic project histories, each about 1,200 words. Authors receive the same sources, task family, 220-word limit and three-field output format. The conventional method explicitly includes strong evidence, uncertainty, dependency and version practices. The two method instructions have equal visible word counts.

Eighteen exact questions are withheld from authors. They address current decisions, evidence distinctions and modest changed conditions. The primary score is a recipient's answer before source retrieval. A separate recovery stage measures what returning to the complete source repairs. Full-source controls test whether the recipient can answer the questions without compression.

The design plans twelve authors, thirty-six handoff recipient contexts, eighteen full-source controls, and at most thirty-six source followups. No such contexts have been launched. One author realization per method/history and three related questions per handoff yield six paired history clusters, not thirty-six independent authoring examples.

The scoring distinguishes a correct decision, support in the actual handoff, faithfulness to the complete source, and honest recognition of missing evidence. This preserves the earlier pilot's finding that a correct requested answer can coexist with an unsupported explanatory claim. It also avoids blaming a recipient for faithfully repeating a source-false statement introduced by the author.

The static preflight checks scope, event references, exact-question exclusion, balanced order, numeric key records and response limits. Those checks are by the same author as the materials; separate source-first key review remains pending. A preparation manifest pins the package. It is not an execution freeze. A bounded recorder must be implemented and rehearsed, and materials review completed, before trials.

**Next:** source-first key review and execution preparation, followed by a separately committed freeze. A tie is an acceptable result. The cases are development-informed synthetic instances, not a demonstration of operational benefit or arbitrary long-context memory.
