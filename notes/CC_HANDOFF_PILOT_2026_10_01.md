## What the preparation establishes

The static check matches ten proposed answers through build-level joins, exact endpoint membership, validation partitions and source availability. Four pairs have identical neutral summaries and queries with different correct answers. Two control summaries already answer their query adequately. There are zero model answers, zero arm comparisons and no evidence of superior handoff performance.

This exposes a useful design distinction. Testing matched-information handoffs evaluates how a recipient uses structure and decides to recover source material. It does not evaluate whether CC authors better summaries from long histories. A condition with more relevant facts would confound those questions. A ceiling result with a strong conventional handoff is a valid result, not a reason to weaken the comparator.
