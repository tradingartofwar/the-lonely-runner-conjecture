# Targeted search scope and limits

Accessed September 29, 2026. This is a bounded source-comparison record, not
an exhaustive bibliography or a negative proof of originality.

The coordinator and reviewers searched the exact paper titles and identifier
records, plus literal variations of Lonely Runner with `5p+2q`, `5p + 2q`,
`5A+2B`, `3a+2b`, `p,q,p+q`, `seven speeds` with `two-parameter`, and
`two segments`. Both available search systems were used. Domain-restricted
queries included arxiv.org, escholarship.org and ams.org.

The direct title/version retrievals produced the primary papers in
SOURCE_REGISTER.json. Formula searches frequently returned irrelevant content
or no result. None identified an exact prior statement of this seven-form
two-segment certificate. Those weak searches do not exclude alternative
notation, parameter changes, signed/permuted presentations or an unindexed
construction. No originality claim follows.

Exact Rosenfeld title plus `mcom`, and `mcom/4243`, retrieved indexed AMS
journal content. It listed the article and August10,2026 electronic publication;
the result URLs themselves were anomalously routed through older DOI addresses.
Direct access to the article DOI failed. This metadata is retained as observed
indexed publisher evidence, separately from the inspected arXiv theorem.

The minimum sufficient general existence source was eight runners. Later
9–15-runner frontier claims were not audited, and references that happened to
surface in search were not used to inflate the comparison's scope.

No outside researcher was contacted and no external certificate was executed.
Future priority work would need a broader equivalence-aware literature search
and/or external mathematical assessment of the specific compact construction.
