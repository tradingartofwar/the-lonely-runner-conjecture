Prepare a concise, well-written project handoff. Identify the current objective, adopted decisions, measured results, unresolved work, dependencies, and next actions. Reconcile earlier messages with later corrections; keep source dates and versions when they affect interpretation. Prioritize facts likely to matter to the incoming coordinator and omit routine background. Preserve important assumptions, exceptions, uncertainty, quantities, and the reasons behind consequential choices. Distinguish proposals from completed actions and observations from expectations. Organize the account so another person can act correctly and explain the current state. Include useful source pointers for details that cannot fit. Consider foreseeable changes within the stated task family and avoid suggesting that a current conclusion applies beyond its supporting evidence.
