# Preserved corrections

Before the reviewers ran the new controls, direct substitution showed that the
protocol's proposed nonprimitive negative fixture (p,q)=(2,4), (x,y)=(7/16,1/4)
has qx-py=5/4. It therefore fails the intended integer premise and cannot
demonstrate the distinction between Z and 2Z. No conclusion is drawn from it.

Analytic replacement: on P3, qx-py=8x-9/4. Setting this equal to 1 gives
(x,y)=(13/32,5/16), which lies on the same safe segment. Now qx-py=1 is an
integer but not in 2Z, while Qx-Py=1/2. This is the declared negative fixture
for the gcd condition. It was derived without a scan. The original protocol
remains unchanged and both fixtures are recorded in the checks.
