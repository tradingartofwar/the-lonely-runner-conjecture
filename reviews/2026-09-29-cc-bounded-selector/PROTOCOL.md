## Question and boundary

For the selected stationary reference among eight common-start runners with
moving speeds A_q=(1,q,q+1,q+2,q+3,2q+3,2q+5), integer q>=2, select one physical
time with every circular distance at least 1/8. Derive from six-form parent
data and the seventh band, without invoking the known seven-form optimum as
a premise. Bound the number of exact rational selection operations independently
of q; allow arithmetic bit lengths and running time to grow with q.

Use the two proposed parent edges P1 and P3, with closed endpoints, the joint
orbit h=qx-y, and physical time t=x. Check the seven affine phase bands, integer
coverage, recovery of physical laps, and the q=4 equality case. Success means
a self-contained proof candidate plus reproducible exact controls, not formal
verification, independent mathematical review, novelty, optimality, all safe
times, all maximizers, another reference runner, or a general Lonely Runner proof.

## Verification scope fixed before execution

1. Verify all seven phase functions at both endpoints of both segments;
   affine interpolation then covers their complete continuous domains.
2. Verify unbounded primary coverage by q>=9 interval width and by eight
   mod-8 affine inequalities. Explicitly account for q=2,...,8 and fallback q=5.
3. Check that both segments are labelled edges of the pinned six-form atlas.
4. Use only the already archived physical inputs q=2,...,25 for direct exact
   selected-time and reflected-time distances and laps. Compare with archived
   optima only after selection; do not rerun an optimizer or extend the scan.
5. Write a separately structured physical-time algebra check, with no selector
   import, certifying the phase inequalities for unbounded residue domains.
   Both programs are authored by this coordinator; structural separation is
   not independent authorship. Check exactly the controls declared above.
6. Pin inputs, scripts and outputs; preserve the old proof/review packages.

Failure means an uncovered q, a failed safe-band inequality, an invalid orbit
or lap recovery, a lost relevant endpoint, or q-dependent candidate enumeration.
Any scope change must be recorded before executing it. No new team review,
broad scan, external contact, literature sweep, main merge or unattended work.
