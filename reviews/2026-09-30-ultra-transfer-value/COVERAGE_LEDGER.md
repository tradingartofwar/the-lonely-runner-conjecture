## What was executed

Written derivations and static read-through are not new execution results.

Inputs/comparators/budgets are written down; implementation source, final prompts/oracles where relevant, and runtime versions must still be frozen before running. The recommended immediate extraction of the three regressions has also not been implemented by this review.
