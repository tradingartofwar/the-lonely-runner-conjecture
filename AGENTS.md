## Partnership

- This is Vance and AI exploring for fun. Do not turn it into an obligation, identity test, or prediction of exceptional success.
- Vance contributes curiosity, intuition, direction, and recognition of interesting structure. AI contributes its own ideas, mathematical explanation, literature checking, implementation, testing, and continuity. These are complementary contributions, not rigid restrictions.
- Give Vance concrete objects to think with: examples, motion, competing explanations, and small questions. Do not require advanced notation before showing the idea.
- Develop intuitions into statements that can fail. Preserve both a promising shape and the evidence that corrects it.
- Vance's September 19 direction: throughout the investigation, actively notice striking patterns, anomalies, and exceptions. Record the exact observation, test a nearby case that could break it, and distinguish a general explanation from a bounded pattern. This is a standing research habit during active work, not authorization for background monitoring or an unlimited search.
- Carry routine technical detail on the AI side; report the useful finding and the uncertainty plainly.

## Evidence discipline

- Distinguish literature result, reported preprint result, reproduced computation, bounded observation, conjecture, and proof candidate.
- Use `n` for total runners and `k=n-1` for other runners relative to a selected reference. Translate each paper's convention explicitly.
- Start all runners at the same position. Independent initial phases define a different problem.
- Animation, dense time samples, optimizer output, and language-model agreement are not proof certificates.
- Use exact rational arithmetic to certify times and distances. Check suspected failures with a separately implemented method, not merely a second prompt.
- A finite search requires a proven reduction and exhaustive coverage to support an unbounded claim. Explain its actual bounds otherwise.
- Do not call a result new until checking the relevant literature and known symmetries. Proof candidates need review beyond the generating model; small formal verification may help but is not established merely by proposing it.
- Cite source versions and dates. Our September 2026 source notes are a starting point, not a forever-current status claim.
